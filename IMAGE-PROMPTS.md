## The direction

This is a precision engineering company running an ISO 9001:2015 quality system, with
modern machinery, exporting internationally. The imagery has to say *exacting* and
*capable*, not *rugged workshop*.

Every prompt below specifies **flawless, brand-new components** and a **modern, clean
facility**. Do not reintroduce words like weathered, worn, grime, patina, micro-scratches or
lived-in — an earlier version of this brief used them and produced parts that looked
second-hand.

Paste this at the **start** of every prompt:

> **STYLE:** High-end industrial product photography for a precision engineering
> manufacturer. Deep near-black studio background with controlled falloff. Every component
> is brand new and flawless: crisp precision-machined surfaces, perfectly uniform
> black-oxide and zinc-plated finishes, sharply formed thread profiles, immaculate edges,
> a clean satin sheen. Lighting is deliberate and controlled — a soft key from the upper
> left, a precise specular highlight running along the top edges, and a warm orange accent
> light grazing one edge and tinting the shadows very slightly warm. Muted charcoal,
> gunmetal and cool steel palette with warm orange as the only saturated colour. Full-frame
> camera, 85mm at f/4 for crisp detail across the subject, photorealistic, high dynamic
> range, very fine grain. Premium, engineered, exacting.

And this at the **end**:

> **AVOID:** any text, letters, numbers, watermarks or logos. No rust, corrosion, pitting,
> scratches, chips, dents, burrs, dirt, dust, grime, oil stains or weathering of any kind.
> No worn, damaged or second-hand looking parts. No clutter, no mess, no old or dilapidated
> machinery, no cracked or stained concrete. No people looking at camera. No stock-photo
> gloss, no HDR halos, no lens flare, no plastic CGI look, no blue-and-teal grading.

**Three rules that matter more than they sound:**

1. **Keep the left third quiet.** Headlines sit there on every hero. Compose so the subject
   is centre-right and the left falls to clean shadow.
2. **Darker than feels right.** These sit under white text with a scrim.
3. **A trace of orange in each frame.** It ties the set to the site's accent. Keep it as a
   light source, never a coloured object. Optional but preferred.

## 1. `hero-home.jpg` — homepage hero · 2560×1440

> [STYLE] A hero arrangement of brand-new forged and machined steel chassis components on a
> dark polished surface: a heavy U-bolt, a spring bush, a centre bolt and a chassis hanger
> bracket. Every part is immaculate, freshly finished, with a deep uniform black-oxide sheen
> and perfectly cut threads. Arranged with generous space between them, precisely placed as
> if for inspection. Low three-quarter angle so the parts feel substantial. The left third
> of the frame is empty clean shadow. A warm orange accent light traces the top edge of the
> nearest U-bolt and washes faintly into the background. [AVOID]

## 2. `craftsmanship.jpg` — full-bleed "Quality starts with the steel" · 2560×1440

> [STYLE] Extreme close-up of a flawlessly machined steel surface, the fine concentric
> turning marks catching a raking light and reading as a precise, even pattern. Mirror-clean
> metal with a cool satin finish and no debris anywhere. Almost abstract, more texture than
> object. A warm orange highlight runs along one crisp machined edge. Composition sweeps
> diagonally from lower left to upper right, the lower left falling to near black. [AVOID]

## 3. `facility.jpg` — the production floor · 2048×2048 (square)

Square because it is cropped two ways — tall on the homepage, wide on the company page.
Keep the subject centred with clear space to every edge.

> [STYLE] The interior of a modern precision manufacturing facility. Rows of enclosed CNC
> turning and machining centres with digital control panels, clean epoxy-resin floor with
> crisp marked walkways, tooling stored in orderly racks, bright even overhead LED lighting
> falling off into shadow at the edges of frame. Immaculate, organised and clearly
> well-invested. A warm orange glow from one machine's status light adds the only saturated
> colour. No people in frame, or one distant figure in clean workwear with their back
> turned. The main subject is centred with empty space toward every edge. [AVOID]

## 4. `materials.jpg` — raw stock · 2000×1600 (5:4)
