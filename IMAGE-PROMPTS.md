# Image brief — Bhatia Auto Engineers

Eight images to generate. Save each one into **`site/public/media/`** using the exact
filename given. The site picks them up automatically on the next refresh; until then each
slot shows a designed gradient panel, so nothing ever looks broken.

**Do not regenerate:** the logo (`public/brand/logo.png`) and the 38 product photographs
in `public/products/`. Those stay exactly as they are.

---

## Read this first: the house style

Every image must look like it came from the same photographer on the same day. Paste this
block at the **start** of every prompt below.

> **STYLE:** Cinematic industrial photography. Deep near-black background (#0A0A0B) falling
> into shadow. A single warm key light from the upper left, and a tight warm-orange rim
> light (#FF6A13) catching one edge of the subject. Shallow depth of field, gentle falloff.
> Real, honest steel: micro-scratches, machining marks, faint oil sheen. Muted palette of
> charcoal, gunmetal and steel blue-grey, with orange reserved as the only saturated
> accent. Shot on a full-frame camera with an 85mm lens, f/2.0. Photorealistic, ultra
> sharp on the subject, high dynamic range, subtle film grain. No text anywhere.

And this at the **end** of every prompt:

> **AVOID:** any text, letters, numbers, watermarks or logos. No people looking at camera.
> No stock-photo gloss, no HDR halos, no lens flare, no plastic CGI look, no blue-and-teal
> colour grading, no cluttered backgrounds, no shiny showroom floors, no visible brand
> names on machinery.

**Two rules that matter more than they sound:**

1. **Keep the left third quiet.** Headlines sit over the left of every hero. Compose so the
   subject sits centre-right and the left side falls into clean shadow or soft gradient.
2. **Darker than feels right.** These sit under white text with a dark scrim on top. An
   image that looks correctly exposed on its own will look washed out on the page.

Export **JPEG, quality 82, sRGB**. Aim to keep each file under 400 KB; resize down to the
listed pixel dimensions before saving.

## When the images are ready

Drop the files into `site/public/media/` and reload. That is all that is required, there is
no code change and no list to update.

Then tell me and I will do a pass over the result: check each image against the text
sitting on top of it, tune the overlay strength per image, and adjust the crops. Generated
images almost never land perfectly against real typography on the first try, and that
tuning is easier for me to do once I can see them.
